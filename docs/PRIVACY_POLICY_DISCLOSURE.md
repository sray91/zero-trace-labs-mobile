# Privacy policy — data collection, use and sharing

Ready-to-publish copy for https://www.0tracelabs.com/privacy-policy, written to satisfy
App Store Guideline 5.1.1(i): the policy must identify **what** data the app collects,
**how** it collects it, **all uses** of that data, and confirm that every third party the
data is shared with provides the same or equal protection.

Everything below is derived from this repo (`convex/schema.ts`, `convex/scanner.ts`,
`convex/supportBot.ts`, `convex/slack.ts`, `convex/pushNotifications.ts`, `convex/http.ts`).
Items marked **[CONFIRM]** cannot be verified from the code — check them before publishing.

---

## What we collect and how

| Data | How it is collected |
|---|---|
| Email address, name, profile image | From you at sign-up, through our authentication provider |
| First and last name, date of birth, phone number | From you, in the onboarding form. Name is required — it is what we search for |
| Street address, city, state, ZIP | From you, in the onboarding form. **Optional** — it improves match accuracy |
| A proxy email address we assign you | Generated by us, not collected from you |
| Scan and removal records: which brokers list you, the URLs of those listings, what was found, removal status and dates, notes and any screenshots | Generated as we scan data-broker sites and submit opt-out requests on your behalf |
| Search history: the name and address combinations used for each scan | Generated when a scan runs |
| Verification emails sent to your proxy address | Routed to us by our email provider when a broker replies |
| Subscription status, plan and renewal date | From Apple and our subscription provider when you purchase or renew |
| Device push token | From your device, if you allow notifications |
| Support conversations: the messages you send us and our replies | From you, when you use the in-app Support chat |

We do not collect or store payment card details. Purchases are handled by Apple.

## How we use it

- To search data-broker sites for listings that match you, and to show you what we found.
- To submit and track opt-out and removal requests on your behalf.
- To receive and read broker verification emails sent to your proxy address, so a removal can
  be confirmed without exposing your real inbox.
- To show your dashboard and removal history.
- To notify you about removal progress and support replies.
- To answer your support questions.
- To manage your subscription and entitlements.
- To secure accounts and prevent abuse.

**[CONFIRM]** We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share only what each provider needs to do its job. Each is bound by a written agreement
requiring it to protect your data to the same or an equal standard as described in this
policy, to use it only to provide the service to us, and not to sell it or use it for its
own purposes.

| Provider | What we send | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthropic, PBC** (Claude API) | The text of your in-app Support chat messages and the assistant's prior replies in that conversation — nothing else. Not your name, email, address, scan results, broker listings, subscription or payment data. | To generate a first-line support answer. Only for users who have accepted the in-app disclosure; you can decline and talk to a person instead, and turn it off at any time in Settings. Under Anthropic's commercial terms these messages are not used to train its models. **[CONFIRM]** |
| **Apify** | Your name, and — if you provided them — your address, phone number and email address. | To run the data-broker search that finds listings about you. This is the core of the service and cannot be turned off separately. |
| **Slack** | Your support messages, and your name and email address, in a private internal channel. | So our support team can read and reply to your conversation. |
| **Clerk** | Your email address, name and profile image. | To create and secure your account and sign you in. |
| **Convex** | All of the data listed above. | It hosts our database and backend. |
| **RevenueCat** and **Apple** | Your subscription identifiers and purchase receipts. | To manage and validate your subscription. |
| **Expo** | Your device push token and the text of the notification, which may include the first 140 characters of a support reply. | To deliver push notifications. |
| **Cloudflare** | Email sent to your proxy address. | To route broker verification emails to us. |

We also disclose personal information to data-broker sites themselves when we submit an
opt-out request, because those requests require enough information to identify your listing.
That is the purpose of the service.

**[CONFIRM]** Legal disclosures, retention periods, your rights (access, deletion, CCPA/GDPR),
how to contact us, and how to delete your account — these belong in the policy but are not
determinable from the app's code.
